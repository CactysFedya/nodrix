- Empty `nodrix init` by default.
- Explicit `core`, `vision`, `media`, `network`, and `benchmark` templates.
- Persistent FFmpeg source and writer nodes.

- Encoded access-unit writer.
- `media doctor`, `probe`, `record`, and `relay`.
- FFmpeg decoder mode in Nodrix Viewer.
